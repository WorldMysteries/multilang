      About the project

      Stories without
      a final page.

      Untold Archive is a collection of websites about stories that have never reached a final line — and about worlds worth returning to.

      One subject, one website. This page is the index: find a story by title, section, or date, then follow the link to read. The originals are published in Russian for now; the English and German editions are reading guides to the same files.

      Every file keeps its source material, its open questions, and its own point of view. No invented endings — only the evidence, the versions, and the space between them.

      “History is not a closed book. It is an archive with missing pages.”
